About Itinerary 360

Itinerary 360 started as a frontend development project at PVPIT (Padmabhooshan Vasantraodada Patil Institute of Engineering & Technology) Sangli, Maharashtra. We saw that while travel websites existed for big international destinations, very few did justice to the rich tapestry of Maharashtra's own backyard.

From the mist-wrapped forts of the Sahyadri to the turquoise waters of the Konkan, from sacred temples to ancient Buddhist caves — Maharashtra is a world unto itself, waiting to be explored. Our goal is to give every traveller a complete 360° picture of what this great state has to offer.

This project was built with care, caffeine, and a genuine love for Maharashtra.
